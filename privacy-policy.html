1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the N Wallet Android app ("the app") and this download website ("the site") collect, use and protect information about you. By installing the app or using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you create an account or use the app, you may provide details such as your name, mobile number or email address, and any information you enter while completing tasks or contacting support.

Information collected automatically. When you use the app, we may collect basic technical information such as device model, Android version, app version, IP address, and logs of activity inside the app (for example, tasks started and completed).

Camera. The camera is accessed only when you use a feature that needs it and only after you grant permission. We do not access the camera in the background.

We do not ask for your UPI PIN, card PIN, OTP or banking passwords inside the app or on this site, and you should never share them with anyone.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To show tasks, record your activity and display your in-app wallet and history.
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse and fix technical problems.
  • To comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. App permissions

  • Internet — to load your account, tasks and activity.
  • Network status — to check whether your phone is online.
  • Camera — only for features that need it, after you allow it. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's Settings.

5. This website and cookies

This download page uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., to measure how many people visit the page and download the app after seeing our ads. The Pixel may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as your browser type, the pages you view and the actions you take (for example, tapping the download button).

You can control how Meta uses this information for ads through your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and you can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Learn more in Meta's Privacy Policy.

6. When we share information

We share information only when needed:

  • With service providers who help us host and run the app (such as cloud hosting), who may only use it to provide those services.
  • With advertising and analytics partners such as Meta, limited to the website data described in section 5.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app.

7. Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices

  • You can view and update your account details inside the app.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by contacting support (see section 11).
  • You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

9. Age requirement

The app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app or site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request deletion of your data, please contact us through the Help section inside the N Wallet app.
